# Architect review: guide compliance and structure (2026-11-08)

**Scope:** UI/UX guide compliance against `docs/ui/UI-UX_GUIDELINES.md`, page structure, navigation, tables and empty states, read from `origin/main` at 2eef35a2. Read-only; no code changed.

**Method:** all 35 route kinds in `App.tsx`, the shared page shell, `PageHeader`, `SectionLayout`, `SortableTable` and the primitives. No browser run: rendered appearance is inferred from source and CSS.

## Verdict

Guide compliance is good but not strict. There are no `UI-EXCEPTION` source markers. Of 19 deviations, 13 are recorded (10 in the guide, 3 only in code or tests) and 6 are unrecorded. 11 are SOUND (a real domain need) and 8 are UNSOUND. All six unrecorded deviations are UNSOUND.

## Consolidation

1. One confirmation dialog on `StagedDialog`, replacing the five `window.confirm` calls, `DeleteConfirmation` (`SavedInterpretationsPanel.tsx:58`) and `ConsultationNotesCard.tsx:153`. Use `variant="danger"`.
2. One tab anatomy shared by `primitives/Tabs.tsx`, `ScrollingTabRow` (`ChartViewNav.tsx`, `AdminNav.tsx`, `SectionNav`), and `app.css:2675` and `:6109`. The guide must first decide the role="tab" question for route sections.
3. A real `ButtonLink` replacing `.chart-pdf-link` (`ChartView.tsx:475`) and the hand-written anchors at `ConsultationView.tsx:473-479` and `People.tsx:117`.
4. A `PageShell` for the not-found, incomplete-moment and unknown-time screens (`PersonNotFound`, `UnreadableSettingsNotice`, the Transit and Progressions copies, and about 12 others by grep).
5. `EmptyState` adoption for the hand-rolled `.hint` empties above.
6. A table action-column helper that sets `align: 'end'` and `sortable: false`, used by the three action columns.
7. Radius tokens for the four literal radii, folded into #1356.
